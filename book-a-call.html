The agenda

What happens in the twenty minutes.

  • You talk for the first tenHow your business runs, how you handle books today, what happened last tax season, and what you wish you could see each month.
  • We tell you what we'd expect to findBased on what you describe, the two or three things most likely leaking money in a business like yours. Specific, not generic.
  • We recommend a tier — or nobodyIncluding "stay with your current bookkeeper" or "you're not big enough yet for this to pay for itself." Both are answers we give regularly.
  • You decide what happens nextUsually the free Profit Leak Audit. No contract discussed on this call, no pressure to decide on the spot.

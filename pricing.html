One-time and optional

The two costs that aren't monthly.

Cleanup Sprint — from $750

Required before monthly service starts. We rebuild the chart of accounts, reconcile back to an agreed starting point, correct miscategorized history and stand up your job costing or commission structure. Typical range is $750–$2,500 depending on how far back the books need work and how many accounts are involved. Quoted after the free audit, fixed once quoted.

Add-ons

Quarterly tax projection for Foundation and Advisor clients, $150 per quarter. Accounts payable and receivable management for contractors juggling subs, $200 per month. Catch-up bookkeeping for prior tax years, quoted per year. We'd rather you skip an add-on you don't need than pay for a bundle you don't use.

The guarantee

On time, or that month is free.

Financials by the 10th, every month, or you aren't billed for that month. It's written into the engagement letter. We guarantee delivery because that's the part we control — we won't promise tax savings or profit outcomes, and you should be wary of firms that do.

Pricing questions

Taxes

No. We're a bookkeeping and advisory firm, not a CPA firm, and we don't file returns or give tax advice. What we do is keep you tax-ready year-round: a running set-aside figure, documented deductions, and a year-end pack your CPA can work from immediately.

We won't promise a number, and be sceptical of anyone who does. What we can say is that deductions you can't substantiate are deductions your CPA can't take — so documenting them as they happen, rather than reconstructing them in March, tends to change the outcome.
